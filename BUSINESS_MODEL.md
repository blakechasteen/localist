# Localist Business Model
**Revenue, Economics, and Growth Strategy**

---

## 💰 Revenue Model

### Hybrid Freemium + Commission Model (Recommended)

**Why Hybrid?**
- Lower barrier to entry for small businesses
- Predictable recurring revenue (subscriptions)
- Performance-based revenue (commissions)
- Alignment with seller success

---

## 📊 Pricing Tiers

### For Businesses (Sellers):

| Tier | Price | Features | Target Segment |
|------|-------|----------|----------------|
| **Free** | $0/month | Basic listing, 1 bulletin post/week, 5 photos, in-app messaging | **Hobby sellers**, new businesses |
| **Local** | $20/month | Everything in Free + unlimited photos, priority in search, analytics dashboard, 2% commission discount | **Small businesses** (1-5 employees) |
| **Community** | $50/month | Everything in Local + featured placement, event hosting, customer CRM, email campaigns, 3% commission discount | **Established businesses** (5-20 employees) |

**Commission** (applies to all tiers):
- **Free tier**: 3% per transaction
- **Local tier**: 1% per transaction
- **Community tier**: 0.5% per transaction

**Transaction Definition**:
- Purchases marked as "completed" by buyer
- Requires seller confirmation
- Commission calculated on total transaction value

---

### For Customers (Buyers):

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Always Free** | $0 | Unlimited discovery, search, messaging, reviews |

**Why Free for Buyers?**
- Maximize network effects (more buyers = more valuable to sellers)
- Compete with Amazon/Walmart (free to use)
- Revenue comes from sellers who benefit directly

## 📈 Unit Economics

### Average Business Profile:

| Metric | Conservative | Realistic | Optimistic |
|--------|--------------|-----------|------------|
| Monthly transactions | 10 | 25 | 50 |
| Average transaction value | $30 | $50 | $100 |
| Monthly GMV (Gross Merchandise Value) | $300 | $1,250 | $5,000 |
| Commission @ 3% | $9 | $37.50 | $150 |
| Subscription revenue | $0 (Free) | $20 (Local) | $50 (Community) |
| **Total monthly revenue per business** | **$9** | **$57.50** | **$200** |

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):

| Channel | CAC | Notes |
|---------|-----|-------|
| **Businesses** (Organic) | $0 | Farmers markets, word-of-mouth |
| **Businesses** (Paid) | $25 | Local ads, business association partnerships |
| **Users** (Organic) | $0 | Referrals, in-store signage |
| **Users** (Paid) | $2 | Instagram ads, local targeting |

### Lifetime Value (LTV):

| Segment | Monthly Revenue | Retention | Lifetime | LTV |
|---------|----------------|-----------|----------|-----|
| **Free Tier Business** | $9 | 50% (6 months) | 6 months | $54 |
| **Local Tier Business** | $57.50 | 75% (12 months) | 12 months | $690 |
| **Community Tier Business** | $200 | 85% (24 months) | 24 months | $4,800 |

**LTV/CAC Ratio**:
- Free tier: $54 / $25 = **2.2× (Acceptable)**
- Local tier: $690 / $25 = **27.6× (Excellent!)**
- Community tier: $4,800 / $25 = **192× (Exceptional!)**

**Profitability**: LTV/CAC > 3× is considered healthy. Localist exceeds this significantly.
