## 📖 User Stories

### Epic 1: Discovery

**As a customer**, I want to discover local businesses near me so that I can support small businesses instead of chains.

#### Stories:

1. **Map-Based Discovery**
   - As Sarah, I want to open a map showing nearby businesses so that I can see what's around me while walking downtown.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Map loads within 2 seconds
     - Businesses appear as pins on map
     - Pins are color-coded by category
     - Tapping a pin shows business preview

2. **Semantic Search**
   - As Mark, I want to search for "artisan coffee" and find local roasters so that I don't have to guess exact business names.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Search returns results within 1 second
     - Results include businesses that don't use exact keywords
     - Results are ranked by relevance + distance
     - Shows 10+ relevant businesses

3. **Radius Filtering**
   - As Linda, I want to filter businesses within 5 miles so that I don't see businesses too far to visit.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Slider to adjust radius (1-50 miles)
     - Map updates in real-time as slider changes
     - Shows count of businesses in radius
     - Can toggle between miles and kilometers

4. **Category Browsing**
   - As Sarah, I want to browse businesses by category (coffee, food, retail, etc.) so that I can explore specific types of businesses.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Categories are clearly displayed (icons + labels)
     - Tapping a category filters map to that category
     - Can select multiple categories (AND logic)
     - Shows business count per category

---

### Epic 2: Business Profiles

**As a customer**, I want to view detailed business profiles so that I can learn about businesses before visiting.

#### Stories:

1. **Business Details**
   - As Mark, I want to see hours, address, and phone so that I know when to visit.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Hours clearly displayed (with "Open now" badge if applicable)
     - One-tap to call or get directions
     - Address shows distance from current location
     - Photos load quickly (< 1 second)

2. **Bulletin Board**
   - As Linda, I want to see weekly specials and updates so that I know what's new.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Bulletin posts show timestamp (e.g., "2 days ago")
     - Photos/text load quickly
     - Posts expire after 1 week (grayed out but visible)
     - Can share post to social media

3. **Product Listings**
   - As Sarah, I want to see products with photos and prices so that I know what to expect.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Products displayed in grid (2 columns)
     - Tapping product shows full details
     - Shows "In Stock" vs. "Out of Stock"
     - Can filter by price range

4. **Reviews**
   - As Mark, I want to read verified reviews from real customers so that I can trust the business.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Reviews show "Verified Purchase" badge
     - Reviews sorted by most recent first
     - Can filter by rating (5-star, 4-star, etc.)
     - Shows reviewer name (first name + last initial)

---

### Epic 3: Messaging

**As a customer**, I want to message businesses so that I can ask questions before visiting.

#### Stories:

1. **Send Message**
   - As Sarah, I want to message a pottery studio asking about class schedules so that I can plan my visit.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Message form is simple (text only for MVP)
     - Send confirmation appears immediately
     - Message delivered within 5 seconds
     - Can attach photo (future: product inquiry)

2. **Receive Response**
   - As Mark, I want to get a notification when a business replies so that I don't miss their response.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Push notification within 1 minute of reply
     - In-app badge shows unread messages
     - Message thread shows history
     - Can archive old conversations

---

### Epic 4: Purchases & Reviews

**As a customer**, I want to mark purchases and leave verified reviews so that I can share my experience with others.

#### Stories:

1. **Mark Purchase**
   - As Linda, I want to mark that I visited and purchased from a business so that I can leave a verified review.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Simple "I purchased from this business" button
     - Timestamp recorded
     - Business receives notification to confirm
     - Can't mark purchase more than once per day

2. **Seller Confirmation**
   - As Tom, I want to confirm customer visits so that only real customers can review.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Notification of customer claim (push + email)
     - One-tap to confirm or deny
     - If no response in 48 hours, auto-confirms
     - Can view pending confirmations in dashboard

3. **Leave Review**
   - As Sarah, I want to leave a 5-star review with text and photos so that I can share my positive experience.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Review form appears after seller confirmation
     - 1-5 star rating (required)
     - Text review (optional, 500 char limit)
     - Photo upload (optional, up to 3 photos)
     - "Submit" button grayed out until rating selected

---

### Epic 5: Business Management (Sellers)

**As a business owner**, I want to manage my profile and connect with customers so that I can grow my business.

#### Stories:

1. **Create Profile**
   - As Emma, I want to create my business profile in 15 minutes so that I can start getting discovered.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Simple form (name, category, description, hours, photos)
     - Geocoding auto-fills location from address
     - Can upload up to 10 photos
     - Preview before publishing

2. **Verification**
   - As Tom, I want to verify my business with Tax ID so that customers trust I'm legitimate.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Upload Tax ID document (photo or PDF)
     - Receives confirmation email
     - Manual review within 48 hours (for MVP)
     - "Verified" badge appears on profile after approval

3. **Bulletin Board Post**
   - As Emma, I want to post weekly specials so that customers see what's new.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Text field (500 chars)
     - Optional photo upload
     - Preview before posting
     - Posts expire after 7 days automatically

4. **Respond to Messages**
   - As Tom, I want to respond to customer messages so that I can answer questions and encourage visits.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Notification within 1 minute of new message
     - Reply form is simple (text only for MVP)
     - Can view conversation history
     - Can archive old conversations

5. **View Analytics**
   - As Maria, I want to see how many people discovered my business so that I can track marketing effectiveness.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Dashboard shows views, searches, messages, purchases
     - Can filter by date range (week, month, year)
     - Export to CSV
     - Shows trend (up/down vs. previous period)

---

### Epic 6: Recommendations

**As a customer**, I want personalized recommendations so that I discover businesses I'll love.

#### Stories:

1. **Recommendation Feed**
   - As Sarah, I want to see businesses similar to ones I've liked so that I discover new favorites.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - Feed shows 5-10 recommendations
     - "Why recommended?" explanation (e.g., "Similar to XYZ Coffee")
     - Can dismiss recommendation ("Not interested")
     - Feed refreshes daily

2. **Thompson Sampling Learning**
   - As Mark, I want the app to learn my preferences so that recommendations improve over time.
   - Acceptance Criteria:
     - System tracks views, visits, purchases
     - Recommendations improve after 5+ interactions
     - Balances familiar (safe) and new (exploration)
     - Doesn't get stuck in filter bubble

## 🎯 User Journeys

### Journey 1: Sarah Discovers a New Pottery Studio

1. **Discovery**: Sarah opens Localist while walking downtown and sees a pottery studio pin on the map (500 feet away).

2. **Exploration**: She taps the pin and views the business profile. Photos show beautiful handmade mugs, and the bulletin board mentions a "wheel-throwing class this Saturday."

3. **Engagement**: She messages the owner asking about class availability.

4. **Response**: The owner replies within 10 minutes confirming space in the class.

5. **Visit**: Sarah visits the studio, takes the class, and buys a handmade mug ($40).

6. **Review**: She marks the purchase in Localist. The owner confirms. Sarah leaves a 5-star review with photos of her mug.

7. **Loyalty**: Localist recommends similar businesses (ceramics stores, art galleries). Sarah becomes a regular customer and discovers 5 new businesses in the next month.

**Outcome**: Sarah spent $200 locally (instead of $50 on Amazon), owner gained a loyal customer, community strengthened.

---

### Journey 2: Tom Hosts a Latte Art Event

1. **Planning**: Tom creates a bulletin board post announcing a free latte art class at his coffee shop (Saturday 10am).

2. **Discovery**: Localist users within 10 miles see the post in their feed. 50 users mark "Interested."

3. **Reminders**: Localist sends push notifications the day before ("Event tomorrow at 10am!").

4. **Turnout**: 30 people attend (20 from Localist, 10 walk-ins).

5. **Engagement**: Attendees post photos on Localist, tag the business, and leave verified reviews.

6. **Growth**: Tom gains 15 new regular customers (who discovered him via the event). His monthly revenue increases by 10%.

7. **Analytics**: Tom views the CRM and sees 30 new customers, 15 with 2+ visits. He sends them a "Thanks for coming!" email with a 10% discount.

**Outcome**: Event cost $200 (coffee, supplies), generated $1,500 in new revenue over next month (7.5× ROI).

### Journey 3: Emma Grows from Side Hustle to Full-Time

1. **Signup**: Emma creates a free Localist profile with photos of her jewelry.

2. **Discovery**: 10 local customers discover her in the first month via map browsing.

3. **Sales**: She makes $500 in local sales (vs. $250/month on Etsy).

4. **Upgrade**: She upgrades to Local tier ($20/month) for better placement and analytics.

5. **Growth**: Over 6 months, she gains 50 regular customers, averages $2,000/month in sales.

6. **Full-Time**: She quits her day job and goes full-time with jewelry-making.

7. **Community**: She collaborates with 3 other Localist makers (cross-promotion, pop-up events).

**Outcome**: Emma builds sustainable local business, creates 2 jobs (hires apprentices), strengthens community.
